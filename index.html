Privacy & Use Policy

Information collected: this demonstration may store the name and Gmail address entered during registration, survey answers, and sign-in timestamps.

Purpose: information is used to demonstrate account access, improve the portal, and show survey feedback to the administrator.

Visibility: in this local demo, records are stored in the current browser only. Anyone with access to this browser may be able to inspect its local data.

Limitations: this file does not verify Gmail ownership, provide secure authentication, or synchronize data across devices. Do not enter sensitive or confidential information.

Choices: users may choose not to answer the optional survey. A production website should provide a verified sign-in system, secure database rules, retention controls, and a complete privacy notice before collecting real personal information.
